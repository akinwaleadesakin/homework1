INSIDE THE CAR

Every Part Has a Purpose.

From the front wing to the tires, every component contributes to the performance of an F1 car.

AERODYNAMIC COMPONENTS

Front & Rear Wings

01

Front Wing

The front wing controls airflow around the front of the car and helps generate downforce. Its design can also affect the aerodynamic performance of the rest of the vehicle.

02

Rear Wing

The rear wing creates downforce and helps provide stability. Its design can be adjusted depending on the characteristics of the circuit.

03

Floor

The floor is one of the most important aerodynamic areas of a modern F1 car because it helps generate downforce using ground-effect principles.

TIRES

Where the Car Meets the Track

Tires are the only parts of the car that directly touch the track, making them extremely important to performance. Formula 1 teams have different tire compounds available for different conditions.

Softer tires generally provide more grip but wear faster, while harder tires tend to last longer. Teams must choose their tires carefully based on weather, track conditions, and race strategy.

DRIVER SAFETY

Protecting the Driver

Safety is one of the most important aspects of modern Formula 1 car design. F1 cars are built with strong carbon-fiber structures and specially designed crash systems to protect the driver.

The halo is one of the most recognizable safety features. It surrounds the driver's cockpit and provides additional protection for the driver's head during serious accidents.
